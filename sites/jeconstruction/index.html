Why Fiber Cement

Built for California Homes

Fiber cement looks like real wood but doesn't rot, warp or feed termites, and it holds paint for years. It's the siding we install most, and the one we recommend.

  • Fire resistant, a big deal in Southern California
  • Won't rot, swell or attract termites
  • Holds paint longer than wood or stucco patch jobs
  • Lap, shingle, board and batten or panel styles
