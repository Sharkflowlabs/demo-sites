Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Sharkflow Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you contact us or use this website, and how we use it.

What we collect

Business contact details you give us or that are publicly listed for your business: your name, business name, phone number, email address, website and social media profile. We also keep records of our messages and calls with you, including call recordings where we tell you a call may be recorded.

How we use it

To prepare your website demo, contact you about it, schedule and remind you of calls, provide our services and keep business records. We do not use your information for any unrelated purpose.

Text messaging

If you agree to receive text messages from Sharkflow Labs, we use your phone number only to send you messages about your website demo, meetings and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We do not sell or share your phone number or text messaging opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the business, such as our phone and messaging provider (Twilio), hosting (Cloudflare) and email. They may only use it to provide their service to us. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

As long as we are in contact with you, and for up to 2 years after, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing hello@sharkflowlabs.com. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
