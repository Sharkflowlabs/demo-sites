How we get your permission

We only text people who have agreed to it. Business owners agree in one of these ways:

  • Submitting the form on this page with the box ticked.
  • Saying yes when we ask, by Instagram message, email or phone: "Can I text you the demo link and a link for our call? It will come from our business number, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP anytime."
