The Classical Method

The original work, taught with care

Classical Pilates follows the system as it was first taught: a set order of exercises that flows from one apparatus to the next and builds strength from the center out. It is precise, it is progressive, and it works.

Whole-body strength

Every session works the body as one connected system.

Taught, not followed

Your teacher watches, corrects and adjusts the work as you go.

The full apparatus

Reformer, tower, chair and more, used the way they were designed.

A practice for life

Build a foundation that keeps you moving well for years.
