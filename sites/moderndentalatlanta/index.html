FAQ

Questions patients ask us.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Book online any time or call our office and we'll find a time that works for you.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most dental insurance plans. Send us your plan details when you book and we'll verify your benefits before your visit.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with healthy gums and enough bone are good candidates. We'll check with an exam and X-rays and talk through your options.

Invisalign or braces: which is right for me?

Invisalign works well for many adults who want a nearly invisible option. Some cases are better with traditional braces. We'll recommend what will get you the best result.

How white will ZOOM whitening make my teeth?

Most patients see a noticeable change in a single visit. Results depend on your starting shade, and we'll tell you what to expect before we begin.
