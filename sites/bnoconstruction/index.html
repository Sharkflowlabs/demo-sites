Our Specialty

Tongue & groove ceilings that change the whole room.

A wood ceiling is the detail people notice first. We install tight, straight tongue & groove on vaulted ceilings, living rooms, porches and lanais, with clean transitions around beams, fans and lighting.

  • Interior vaulted and flat ceilings
  • Porch & lanai wood ceilings built for Florida weather
  • Pine, cedar and stained finishes to match your home
  • Beams, recessed lighting and fan cut-outs done right
