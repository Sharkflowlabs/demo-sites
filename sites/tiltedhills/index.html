What We Do

Everything between bare walls and move-in ready.

From a single accent wall to a full cottage refresh, we handle the prep, the repairs and the finish so you only deal with one crew.

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, doors and trim. Careful prep, crisp cut lines and full floor protection in every room.

Drywall & Repairs

New drywall, patches, cracks and taping, sanded smooth and ready for paint so the damage disappears.

Trim Replacement

Out with dated trim, in with clean step bevel MDF baseboards and casings, installed, caulked and painted.

Exterior Painting

Siding, brick, soffits and porches refreshed to stand up to Haliburton winters and lake-side sun.

Decks & Staining

Decks, porches and wood siding cleaned, stained and sealed so the wood looks great and lasts longer.

Accent Walls & Colour

Bold feature walls, board and batten and moody colours. Not sure what works? We'll help you choose.
