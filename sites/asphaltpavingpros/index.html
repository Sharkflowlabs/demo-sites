FAQ

Common Questions

How much does an asphalt driveway cost?

It depends on size, the condition of the base and how much grading is needed. We come out, measure, and give you a clear written price for free.

Should I repair or repave?

If the base is still solid, patching and sealcoating can buy you years for much less money. If it's broken up across large areas, repaving is the better long-term deal. We'll tell you honestly which one makes sense.

How often should asphalt be sealcoated?

Most driveways and lots do best with a fresh sealcoat every 2 to 3 years. It keeps water, sun and oil from breaking the surface down.

How soon can I drive on new asphalt?

Usually after 2 to 3 days for cars, longer in hot weather. We'll give you care tips at the final walkthrough.

Do you do commercial parking lots?

Yes. We pave, patch and sealcoat parking lots for businesses, property managers and HOAs, and can schedule work to keep your lot open where possible.
