What we do

Painting services for Brevard homes

From a single room to a full exterior repaint, we prep properly, protect your home, and leave it cleaner than we found it.

Exterior painting

Stucco, siding, trim, doors and garage doors, with coatings that stand up to Florida heat, humidity and salt air.

Interior painting

Walls, trim, doors and ceilings. Sharp cut lines, furniture and floors protected, and a tidy cleanup every day.

Cabinet painting

Give your kitchen or bathroom a fresh, durable finish for a fraction of the cost of new cabinets.

Wood staining

Decks, fences, pergolas and doors, stained and sealed to bring out the grain and protect against sun and rain.

Pressure washing

Driveways, walkways, pool decks and walls. The right first step before paint, or a quick refresh on its own.

Ceiling painting

Popcorn-free or smooth, high or vaulted, we handle ceilings cleanly so the whole room looks new.
