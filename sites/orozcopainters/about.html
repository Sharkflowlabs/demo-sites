Who We Are

Meet Cristian Orozco

I'm Cristian Orozco, the owner of Orozco Painters Solutions Inc. I started the company after working hands-on in the painting trade. I wanted to build a business that takes the time to protect each space, prep surfaces properly, and pay attention to the details our clients will see every day.

We're based in San Marcos, California, and work on homes and commercial properties. One of our most meaningful projects has been painting a home that's more than 100 years old. Working on its original woodwork and fireplace is a good example of the care we bring to a job and how we help preserve the character that makes a place special.
