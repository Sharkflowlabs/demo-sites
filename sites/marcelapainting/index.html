FAQ

Questions We Get A Lot

How much does it cost to paint a house exterior?

It depends on the size of the home, the condition of the stucco and how much prep is needed. We'll come out, look at the house and give you a clear written price for free.

Do you pressure wash and repair cracks before painting?

Yes. Washing, patching stucco cracks and priming are part of every exterior job. Skipping prep is the number one reason paint fails in Florida.

Can you paint my kitchen cabinets instead of replacing them?

Absolutely. We mask the whole kitchen, clean, sand and prime the boxes and doors, then spray for a smooth finish. It's a fraction of the cost of new cabinets.

How long does a typical job take?

Most interiors take a few days and most exteriors about a week, depending on size and weather. We'll give you a timeline with your estimate.
