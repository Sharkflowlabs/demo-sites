FAQ

Questions patients ask us.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Request a time online or call (404) 589-0900 and we'll find a time that works for you.

Do you take my insurance?

We bill all traditional dental insurance plans and now accept VA dental insurance. Share your plan when you book and we'll verify your benefits first.

What if I don't have insurance?

We offer in-house and third-party financing with same-day approvals, and accept CareCredit and most major credit cards.

I get nervous at the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. We offer light to mild sedation options and we'll go at your pace, explaining everything before we start.

What should I do in a dental emergency after hours?

Call our after-hours line at (470) 303-9047.
